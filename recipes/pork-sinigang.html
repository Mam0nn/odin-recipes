Pork Sinigang

Description

Pork sinigang is a comforting Filipino soup known for its rich, savory broth and distinct sour flavor. Made with tender pork, fresh vegetables, and tamarind-based seasoning, it is a hearty dish that's perfect with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork belly or pork shoulder, cut into pieces
  • 1 liter water
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 2 medium tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 packet sinigang mix
  • 1 cup radish, sliced
  • 1 cup string beans, cut into pieces
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 1 cup kangkong leaves and stems
  • 2 green chili peppers
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the pork: Place the pork and water in a large pot. Bring to a boil and remove any scum that rises to the surface.
  2. Add the aromatics: Add the onion and tomatoes. Cover and simmer for about 30–40 minutes, or until the pork becomes tender.
  3. Add the vegetables: Add the radish and eggplant. Cook for about 5 minutes, then add the string beans and green chili peppers.
  4. Season the broth: Add the sinigang mix and fish sauce. Stir well and adjust the seasoning with salt and black pepper.
  5. Add the kangkong: Add the kangkong leaves and stems. Cook for another 2–3 minutes until the vegetables are tender but still fresh.
  6. Serve: Turn off the heat and serve the sinigang hot with steamed white rice.
