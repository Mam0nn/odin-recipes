EGG FRIED RICE

Description

Egg fried rice is a simple and flavorful dish made with fluffy rice, scrambled eggs, garlic, and green onions. Stir-fried with soy sauce and a touch of seasoning, it is quick to prepare and makes a delicious meal or side dish.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice, preferably cold
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons chopped green onions
  • 1/4 cup diced carrots or peas (optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare the eggs: Beat the eggs in a small bowl and set aside.
  2. Heat the pan: Heat the cooking oil in a wok or large pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook the eggs: Add the beaten eggs and scramble until just cooked. Remove and set aside.
  4. Sauté the garlic: Add a little more oil if needed, then sauté the minced garlic until fragrant.
  5. Add the rice: Add the cold cooked rice. Break up any large clumps and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Season: Add soy sauce, salt, and black pepper. Mix thoroughly.
  7. Add the eggs: Return the scrambled eggs to the pan and mix everything together.
  8. Finish: Add the green onions and optional vegetables. Stir-fry for another 1–2 minutes.
  9. Serve: Transfer to a plate and serve hot.
