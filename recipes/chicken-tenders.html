CHICKEN TENDERS

Description

Chicken tenders are crispy, golden strips of tender chicken coated in seasoned breadcrumbs and fried until perfectly cooked. They're easy to make and are great served with your favorite dipping sauce, fries, or a fresh salad.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast, cut into strips
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • Cooking oil, for frying

Steps

  1. Prepare the chicken: Cut the chicken breast into even-sized strips.
  2. Prepare the coating: Place the flour in one bowl. Beat the eggs in a second bowl. In a third bowl, combine the breadcrumbs, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and onion powder.
  3. Coat the chicken: Dip each chicken strip into the flour, then into the beaten eggs, and finally coat it evenly with the seasoned breadcrumbs.
  4. Heat the oil: Heat about 1–2 inches of cooking oil in a deep pan over medium heat.
  5. Fry the chicken: Carefully place the coated chicken strips into the hot oil. Fry for about 3–4 minutes on each side, or until golden brown and fully cooked.
  6. Drain: Remove the chicken tenders and place them on a plate lined with paper towels to remove excess oil.
  7. Serve: Serve hot with ketchup, mayonnaise, ranch, honey mustard, or your favorite dipping sauce.
